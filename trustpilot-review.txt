Account verification left my trial VPS unrecoverable

A security verification check ended with my seven-day trial VPS cancelled. Spaceship restored my account access after verification, but support said it could not restore the server or recover its data.

I registered on 3 November 2026. Only a few hours into the trial, I ordered an additional paid VPS. Within a minute, my account was restricted.

The agent initially answered yes when asked whether the restriction was an error. He later explained that automated security checks can temporarily restrict accounts, said this did not mean I was suspected of fraud, and declined to disclose the trigger. He explicitly linked the VPS cancellation to that process. I include both answers in the evidence.

After checking with a supervisor, support declined to restore the interrupted trial or provide a replacement. The explanation included: “However, this is how our system works, and it cannot be changed.” In the final exchange, the agent again confirmed that VPS access could not be restored.

The agent was courteous, and some results had already been backed up independently. But there was no practical remedy for the server itself. My concern is why a temporary verification process resulted in cancellation of a VPS that could not be restored, rather than a reversible suspension. That leaves me reluctant to trust the service with an important project.
